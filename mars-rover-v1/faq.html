Frequently Asked Questions

Below are some common questions about this educational project and the Mars rover content. Expand a question to read the answer.

Who created this website?

This site was created by John Gray as a student project for educational purposes. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.

Where does the mission data and imagery come from?

Images and mission data used on this site are adapted from NASA public-domain resources and mission archives when applicable. Sources are acknowledged where used.

Can I reuse the images on this site?

Most NASA mission imagery is in the public domain and may be reused with appropriate attribution. Third-party images may have restrictions — check the image caption or source for licensing details.

Why does the site say NASA is not affiliated with this work?

This is an independent student project. While the site uses public-domain NASA resources for educational purposes, NASA did not create, review, or endorse this website.

How do I report a problem or typo?

Click the Report a problem link in the footer to report issues. You can also email the site author if an address is provided.

Is this content suitable for classroom use?

Yes. The content is designed to be accessible and educational for students and instructors. If you plan to use this material in a classroom, feel free to adapt it with proper attribution.
